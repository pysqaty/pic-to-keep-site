Pic to Keep

Privacy policy

5 November 2026

Pic to Keep is made by Dawid Mastalerz.

Photos stay on your device

The app reads an album you choose, and on a Mac a folder you choose, so it can group similar shots and suggest which to keep. Scoring happens on that device. Photo files are not uploaded. The app does not delete photos. If you save rejects, it creates a new Photos album of references, or on a Mac a folder of Finder aliases. The originals remain in place.

What is sent off the device

The app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, using PostHog's EU cloud at eu.i.posthog.com. There is no account. The events can include:

  • a random identifier created on the device
  • whether the device is an iPhone, iPad, or Mac
  • app version, device type, and system version
  • actions such as opening the guide, refreshing or filtering albums, starting a comparison, marking keep or reject, saving rejects, and starting or finishing scoring
  • counts, such as how many photos were in a run, how many were rejected, and how long scoring took
  • the IP address of the connection, which PostHog may use to estimate a coarse location such as a country

The events do not include your photos, album names, or folder names. They are used to understand which parts of the app people use. They are not used to advertise, and they are not sold. PostHog processes them on the developer's behalf. Session replay is off.

The app does not ask you to track activity across other companies' apps and websites.
